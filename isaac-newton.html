Isaac Newton: Nothing, Nobody Comes Close

by Iasin H. Shishir

Who do you think is the most extraordinary scientific mind that humanity has produced?

American astrophysicist Neil deGrasse Tyson was asked this question, to which he replied,

There is no contest. Isaac Newton. Nothing, nobody comes close.

He, working alone, discovers the laws of motion. Then he discovers the law of gravity, universal law of gravitation.

Then someone asks, "Why do your planets orbit in this shape - ellipses, ovals - rather than perfect circle?"

He says, "I don't know, I'll get back to you."

He goes home and he comes back, "I finally have my answer."

They say, "Isaac, how did you do that?"

"Well, I had to invent integral and differential calculus to answer that question."

He then discovers the laws of optics, deducing that white light is composed of colors. Because you could take those same colors, recombine them and get white light, which freaked out artists of the day.

He does all of this, then he turns 26.
